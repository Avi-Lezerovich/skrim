- **Conflict with the task brief:** the brief says a poll failing "respondents ≤ contacts" stays out until approved. The repo rule (warn = not blocking) wins, and I'm reporting it here.

## 6. Publication ban (§16ה(ח))

**Rule:** the official text the CEC publishes (נוסח סעיף 16ה, updated 08.08.2022, [gov.il PDF](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/guide/knesset25-elections-info/he/survey_section16_acces.pdf)) says:

> «בתקופה שתחילתה בתום יום שישי שלפני פתיחת הקלפיות והמסתיימת במועד סגירת הקלפיות»

During that period no one may publish results of a poll that was not published before it began.

- **Start:** end of Friday 23.10.2026, i.e. 24:00 on Friday into Saturday 24.10. [Maariv, 12.10.2026](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/elections-2026/article-1376327) gives the same date.
- **End:** when polls close on 27.10.2026. The 22:00 closing time is **[unverified]**: I did not check it against an official source.
- **[unverified]:** I could not confirm that no amendment after 08.08.2022 changed this.

Who may publish an older poll during the period: anyone republishing a poll published before it began must state prominently that the poll is not current and says nothing about voting patterns or public opinion on the day of publication.

**Is the site affected?** Yes. It is live, and PR #12 records your decision to keep it up through the blackout. For your decision:

- **New polls.** Any filing not already public before Friday 24:00 must not reach the site until polls close. The scrape cron only commits the listing and doesn't redeploy, but a manual update and deploy on 24–27.10 would publish such polls. The CEC's `published_date` is when the CEC posted the filing, not when the poll was broadcast, so the site can't prove a poll was public before the cutoff.
- **Existing polls.** The statute requires a prominent "not current" notice while they stay up. The live site no longer has a banner.
- **Averages, trends and share images.** These are an analysis combining older polls. Whether they count as "results of an election poll" is a legal question, also open in SPEC.md.
- **Accuracy score.** methodology.md still says «[לאמת … ככל הנראה יום שישי 23.10.2026]». The statute now confirms the cutoff. The text needs your approval before 27.10.
