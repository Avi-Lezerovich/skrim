## 5. Publication ban (§16ה(ח))

All eight filings were conducted by 22.10, but the CEC posted them on Sunday 25.10, inside the blackout that began at the end of Friday 23.10. The 23.10 report said nothing posted from 24.10 should reach the site without the owner's decision. The owner asked for this update on 25.10. Merging this PR deploys it.

There are two dates here. According to the owner, the media published these polls by Friday 23.10, before the blackout. The CEC put the filings on its site only on Sunday 25.10, and that is the `public_to_cec` date shown above. The site adds no new polls. It shows polls that were already public, with links to their CEC filings.
