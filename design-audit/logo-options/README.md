# Logo directions (proposals — nothing is wired in)

All shapes are hand-written paths (no font). Accent = `--link` (#1a56a8 light / #8ab8ff dark). Each sheet shows the lockup in light and dark, a monochrome lockup, and the mark at 64/32/24/16px.

- **1-band.svg — Band — the site's chart grammar in a tile.** Average line inside its spread band with poll dots around it, on an accent tile: says 'poll average with uncertainty' and grows out of the current favicon.
- **2-estimate.svg — Estimate — a point with its interval.** One dot with a band and whiskers: the honest unit of a poll (a number plus how unsure it is). It also becomes the dot on the i, so mark and wordmark share one idea.
- **3-slot.svg — Slot — a ballot box whose slot holds a trend.** Box outline with the slot notch at the top and a rising line ending in a dot: polls exist to anticipate the ballot, and the dot sits in the slot.

Regenerate: `node design-audit/logo-options/make.mjs`
